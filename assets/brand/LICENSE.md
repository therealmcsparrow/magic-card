## Files that contain Material Design Icons shapes

Some files contain icon shapes of [Material Design Icons](https://pictogrammers.com/library/mdi/), maintained by Pictogrammers and taken from the `@mdi/js` package. Those shapes stay under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). They are in these files:

- The five rigs in `icon-library/`: `bat-rig.svg`, `cctv-rig.svg`, `skull-rig.svg`, `string-lights-rig.svg` and `wind-turbine-rig.svg`. Each is cut from the Material Design Icons shape of the same name. The shape was split into separate pieces so that parts can move on their own. These rigs are modified versions of the original shapes, and this note states that change as the Apache License 2.0 asks.
- The two boards in `art-direction/`, `directions-board.svg` and `styles-board.svg`, and their PNG exports `directions-board.png` and `styles-board.png`. They show Material Design Icons shapes. The board text credits the shapes to Material Design Icons by Pictogrammers (Apache-2.0).

In these files, the Apache License 2.0 covers the Material Design Icons shapes. The parts made for this project (the board layout, the part groupings and the text) stay under the MIT licence.

If you copy or share any of these files, or a piece of one, keep this note with it. That keeps the Apache License 2.0 attribution to Pictogrammers and the pointer to the licence text with the shapes. The package ships no NOTICE file. The licence statement from the package and the full licence text are in [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](../../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md), and the README credits the same source.

Pictogrammers does not endorse Magic Card, and the Material Design Icons name is used only to say where the shapes come from.
